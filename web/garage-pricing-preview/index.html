Frequently asked questions

Do I pay a commission on bookings?

No. The proposed Garage subscription includes 0% Flotaryx commission per request, confirmed appointment or completed repair.

What is included in the subscription?

One physical workshop location with the planned features and service bay limit of your tier. No charge per staff login.

Are prices including VAT?

Prices shown are before applicable tax. The final invoice depends on your business billing address, tax registration and tax rules.

Is a customer request a confirmed booking?

No. A preferred time is only a request until the workshop explicitly confirms it.

How does annual billing work?

One advance payment for 12 months at the cost of 10 monthly payments. Renewal and cancellation terms will be clear before checkout.

Can I register and pay now?

Not yet. This is a non-purchasable design preview. Garage onboarding and paid subscriptions will open only after QA and launch approval.
