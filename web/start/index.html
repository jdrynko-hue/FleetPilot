Frequently asked questions

What to know before you begin.

Is Flotaryx only for delivery vans?

No. Core vehicle, driver, inspection, defect and maintenance records work across vehicle categories. Industry-specific processes may still require specialised systems.

Can drivers use Flotaryx on their phones?

Yes. Drivers have a phone-friendly interface for an assigned vehicle, pre-trip and post-trip checks and defect reporting.

Is there an app to install?

Flotaryx can be installed as a web app from a supported phone browser. A native App Store or Google Play listing is not yet available.

How will Flotaryx develop?

We are exploring future GPS and telematics integrations and more sector-specific tools. Their scope and release dates have not yet been confirmed.

Can companies have separate workspaces?

Yes. Company workspaces have separate records and role-based access. Team members can be invited through a shareable link.

Can Flotaryx replace legal transport compliance tools?

No. Legal requirements such as tachograph, taxi licensing and other sector-specific obligations remain the operator's responsibility. Flotaryx is an operational management tool.
