Frequently asked questions

What to know before you begin.

Is FleetPilot only for delivery vans?

No. Core vehicle, driver, inspection, defect and maintenance records work across vehicle categories. Industry-specific processes may still require specialised systems.

Can drivers use FleetPilot on their phones?

Yes. Drivers have a phone-friendly interface for an assigned vehicle, pre-trip and post-trip checks and defect reporting.

Is there an app to install?

FleetPilot can be installed as a web app from a supported phone browser. A native App Store or Google Play listing is not yet available.

Does FleetPilot track vehicles with live GPS?

No. The current product focuses on records, driver checks, defects, repairs and fleet visibility, not live location tracking or route dispatch.

Can companies have separate workspaces?

Yes. Company workspaces have separate records and role-based access. Team members can be invited through a shareable link.

Can FleetPilot replace legal transport compliance tools?

No. Legal requirements such as tachograph, taxi licensing and other sector-specific obligations remain the operator's responsibility. FleetPilot is an operational management tool.
